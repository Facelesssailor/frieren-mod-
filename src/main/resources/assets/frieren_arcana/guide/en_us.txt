§2§lLearning and
Casting§r
All spells in this book
are Iron's Spells 'n
Spellbooks spells.
Find or craft their
scrolls, scribe them
into a spellbook at an
Inscription Table,
equip the book and
cast with Iron's §lCast
Selected Spell§r key
(V by default) or the
@@page
spell wheel (R).

Each spell has a
minimum rarity: a
scroll of lower rarity
cannot hold it. Higher
spell levels raise
damage, radius and
duration and cost a
little more mana.

Spells with a §lLong§r
cast keep you casting
@@page
for their cast time
first. §lToggle§r spells
turn on with one cast
and off with the next.
Barrage stops when
you cast it again,
release right-click
with a staff, or press
Stop.

Tooltips in the
spellbook show each
spell's damage at
@@page
your current spell
power.

§2§lKeybindings§r
§lFrieren: Magic§r
category in Controls:

- §lN§r Cycle Zoltraak
mode (single, barrage,
heavy)
- §lY§r Cast or toggle
the selected Zoltraak
mode
- §lB§r Stop the barrage
or cancel a heavy
charge
@@page
- §lK§r or Esc: skip a
spell cutscene (the
spell still happens)
- §lU§r Throw the target
of Object Levitation

In big modpacks
several mods may
want the same key.
NeoForge gives a key
press to only one of
them, so a clash with
@@page
Iron's cast key can
stop spells from
casting at all.

The first time you
join a world, this mod
moves any default
key that clashes with
Iron's cast, spell
wheel or quick-cast
keys, and any of its
own keys that clash,
to a free key, and
@@page
tells you in chat what
moved. Keys you set
yourself are never
changed. Delete
§oconfig/frieren_arcan
a-keyfix.txt§r to run it
again.

§2§lSpell Power and
Damage§r
Spell power for these
spells is 8 at level 1,
plus 3 for each level
after, multiplied by
your Iron's spell
power and school
power bonuses from
gear and attributes.

Each spell's damage
is a multiple of that
@@page
power, shown on its
page and in its tooltip.
Magic resistance and
Iron's own damage
rules apply as usual.

Physical spells
(Balgrant, Doragate
and the golem fist)
count as stronger
against defensive
magic, as in the anime:
defensive barriers
@@page
are weak to mass.

Terrain and barriers
block beams. Spells
never grief terrain
unless noted.

§2§lCutscenes§r
Two spells play a
short cinematic for
the caster: the
§lBarrier Breaker§r
(about 25 s) and the
§lBlack Hole§r (about 8
s). While one plays,
your movement and
mouse are locked so
the camera cannot be
dragged.

@@page
Press §lK§r or §lEsc§r to
skip at any time.
Skipping only ends the
camera: the spell still
happens exactly the
same for everyone.

§2§lSpell Circles§r
Casting draws a magic
circle in the anime's
style: a double outer
ring, a band of
ancient script, a fine
tick ring and node
circles, with a figure
in the middle that
shows the spell's
family.

Aimed spells draw it in
@@page
front of you, area
spells on the ground,
Catastravia overhead
and Judradjim high in
the sky above its
target. The golem's
circle is a violet
sunburst that opens
under the target
before the fist
breaks through.
